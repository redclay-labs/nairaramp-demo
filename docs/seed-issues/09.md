### Context
In `app/page.tsx`, `DepositSection.tsx`, and `WithdrawSection.tsx`, users enter an amount to deposit or withdraw without knowing the conversion rate or estimated receive amount until the anchor popup opens. The `@nairaramp/core` SDK exports `ramp.getPrice({ sellAsset, buyAsset, sellAmount })` (documented in `upstream/core-API.md`), which fetches SEP-38 indicative rates from the anchor. Adding a rate calculator provides transparent, real-time conversion estimates.
